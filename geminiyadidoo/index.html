The Fragmentation Paradox

The challenge in the ICU is not a scarcity of sensors—it is assembling time under alarm pressure.

When caring for mechanically ventilated adults, clinicians must synthesize evolving trends across separate devices while managing frequent threshold-based alarms (Nasirmoghadas et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2024). PulseMind replaces manual mental stitching with a disciplined Two-Level Review Pathway.

PROPOSED WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

The Two-Level Review Pathway

Separating unit-wide triage from deep patient inspection prevents cognitive overload while keeping existing bedside alarms intact.

LEVEL 01 // UNIT-WIDE TRIAGE
Prioritised Patient Overview Board

Organizes ventilated ICU beds by review state (Pending Review ranked before Reviewed) and target-specific risk level, with explicit flags when data sufficiency is limited or unknown.

LEVEL 02 // PATIENT & PARAMETER DEEP-DIVE
Time-Aligned Trajectory & Provenance

Synchronizes ventilator, physiological, and lab timelines; ranks model-contributing variables; surfaces source-traceable explanations; and distinguishes Measured vs. Carried-Forward readings.

Hypothesis H1 & H2

Assembly Time & Early Consistency

A time-aligned view of ventilator, physiological, and lab data may reduce manual information-assembly time without reducing review accuracy, supporting earlier and more consistent identification of patients requiring review.

Hypothesis H3

Selective Prompting Without Fatigue

Applying strict target-specific criteria (High or Critical only) before displaying a focused in-interface review prompt may support prioritisation without creating an unacceptable additional attention burden.

Hypothesis H4 & H5

Calibrated Reliance via Provenance

Explicit data-quality limitations, unavailable-data states, and ranked model-contributing factors may help clinicians recognise when an assessment should not be relied upon and why a case was prioritised.

How to use PulseMind

Reopened from your profile menu. Nothing on this page changes what you are looking at.

What PulseMind is

It shows a respiratory-risk assessment for the adult ventilated patients on your unit, so you can decide who to look at first. It is read-only: nothing you do here is written to the patient record, no order is placed, and no clinician is notified.

It supports your review. It does not make decisions, and it does not replace looking at the patient.

The three screens

  1. Patient overview — the whole unit, ranked. Selecting a card shows a summary panel beside the board; Open patient detail is what actually opens the patient. Selecting and opening are two separate actions on purpose.
  2. Patient detail — one patient: the current score and its risk level, what is driving it, the ventilator parameters from the latest reading, and the review panel.
  3. Parameter detail — one parameter over the recent window, and where each value came from (Measured, Carried forward, or Population reference).

How the board is ordered

Patients needing review come first, then by risk level, then by risk score. Missing or uncertain information is shown through explicit states such as RISK LEVEL UNAVAILABLE or DATA SUFFICIENCY UNKNOWN rather than being left blank.

Three-Component Data Integrity Clarification

Every prediction is accompanied by three explicit integrity metrics so clinicians never mistake imputed fallbacks or charting habits for true physiology:

01. Physiology Share

Proportion of input features reflecting direct, freshly measured physiological & ventilator signals from the patient.

02. Documentation Share

Accounts for whether input density reflects clinician charting frequency/writing habits rather than acute biological change.

03. Imputed Share

Aggregate share of blank inputs filled via population mean/reference fallback so the model has non-empty vectors.

POST-PREDICTION RAG EXPLANATION LAYER: Operates strictly after numerical risk estimation. Converts structured contributing factors into concise clinical prose with version-controlled medical citations—and cannot modify or override the model's risk score.

Section 7.1 // Evidence Before Deployment

The next value-creating milestone is not a live deployment—it is a locked validation protocol.

PulseMind advances through six conditional gates. Each phase resolves a specific clinical, data, or integration uncertainty—and halts or iterates if predefined safety and performance criteria are not met.

PHASE 01 · CURRENT STAGE

Public-Data Proof of Concept

Define initial respiratory target & horizon; benchmark ETL and XGBoost on de-identified MIMIC-IV data; connect to the 3-screen clinician review prototype.

GATE: Provenance & reproducibility documented; no patient-care use.
PHASE 02 · NEXT MILESTONE

Vietnamese Data Evaluation

Test Phase 1 model on approved, de-identified historical data from a partner Vietnamese adult ICU without live connection; refine only if required.

GATE: Hospital ethics/data governance approval & locked protocol.
PHASE 03 · TECHNICAL MVP

Live Integration MVP

Validate real-time data export from compatible ICU equipment (e.g., HAMILTON-C6/S1 or Capsule MDIP) testing format, latency, and reliability.

GATE: End-to-end Edge Connector & cybersecurity verification.
PHASE 04 · MODEL LOCK

Primary Dataset & Training

Link validated live measurements from Vietnamese ICU patients with confirmed respiratory events to train, calibrate, and lock the primary model.

GATE: Locked model weights, calibration & threshold verification.
PHASE 05 · CLINICAL PILOT

Shadow Mode & Pilot

Evaluate fixed model on live data with outputs hidden from clinicians first; progress to controlled clinician review pilot only after safety checks pass.

GATE: Audit logging, downtime/study-stop rules & regulatory clearance.
PHASE 06 · COMMERCIAL

Staged ICU Deployment

Prepare quality management, post-deployment monitoring, security updates, and site-specific onboarding across qualified hospital accounts.

GATE: Market authorisation & evaluation-to-licence conversion.
