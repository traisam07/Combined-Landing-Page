Create an exceptionally beautiful sculptural 3D rendering of a pair of human lungs and short trachea, in translucent deep crimson-red glass with flowing hair-thin red fiber-optic bronchial branches visible inside. Elegant front three-quarter view, a little tilted, two clearly identifiable organically shaped lung lobes. The lungs should feel like a futuristic museum sculpture, with delicate filament detail, fine red particles, intricate caustics, ruby glass edges and soft white highlights; never gory, never fleshy. Isolated centrally on a completely clean near-white studio background (#faf9f6), with a very soft subtle contact shadow beneath. Restrained lighting, high-end medical technology art direction, refined scientific beauty. Full subject visible with some breathing room around, centered composition, square image 1536x1536. All reds rich crimson (around #bd1939), no orange, no blue, no purple. No text, no labels, no circles, no UI, no watermark. This should be a striking polished art object with lots of fine fiber-like details and refractive glass surface, not a diagram and not a realistic wet organ.
