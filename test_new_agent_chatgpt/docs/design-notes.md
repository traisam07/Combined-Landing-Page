# Design direction

## References researched

- [Awwwards healthcare and fitness collection](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/health-fitness/) — a survey of healthcare identities, avoiding the usual stock-photo and generic blue dashboard pattern.
- [Heva Health’s Awwwards entry](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/heva-health) and [current website](https://www.hevahealth.com/) — spacious hierarchy, an approachable healthcare voice, and a restrained palette. The current site differs from the original awarded design.
- [Lusion](https://lusion.co/) — sculptural imagery, strong typographic contrast, and purposeful interaction that turns a concept into an experience.

These are conceptual references. No artwork, code, layouts, or claims were copied.

## PulseMind’s identity

The central visual metaphor is many signals becoming one patient story. A crimson glass-lung sculpture makes the respiratory focus immediately recognizable; fine internal branches echo the connected information theme. A custom animated canvas connects the vision to the product demonstration. A full crimson section signals the transition from the idea to the experience.

The page alternates spacious light sections with a concentrated red product section and an oversized closing invitation. Manrope supplies clear, contemporary typography; IBM Plex Mono gives small labels a measured, scientific character. Both are self-hosted. The pulse mark and data graphics are original SVG/code assets.

The interactive demonstration replaces a static screenshot: visitors can search patients, select a case, inspect chart ranges, trace the source, and mark a case reviewed. Native dialogs and keyboard-enabled tabs provide equivalent access without relying on hover or motion.

## Evidence boundaries

- Survey Result.md is the source of truth for statistics.
- 36 is the total eligible sample, not a number of customers or endorsers.
- 4.25 is the average number of source groups selected, not a measured efficiency burden.
- 77.8% is conditional concept usefulness, not validated usability or clinical effectiveness.
- The landing page distinguishes an illustrative prototype from future validated clinical functionality.
- The original source documents were kept outside the public site.
